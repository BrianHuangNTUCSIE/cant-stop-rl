`vmerckle_monte` and `vmerckle_monte_uct` are clean-room implementations based
on behavioral inspection of commit
`25be22910e3bfc5c351f38ff5461602d95dacd0a`. The repository had no explicit
license at that commit, so its source was not copied or redistributed.

## Medeiros et al. Sketch learning

The existing `couto` adapter does not redistribute source files from the
MIT-licensed reference repository. Attribution: Leandro C. Medeiros, David S.
Aleixo, and Levi H. S. Levis, “What Can We Learn Even From The Weakest? Learning
Sketches for Programmatic Strategies,” AAAI 2022; reference:
https://github.com/leandrocouto/sketch-learning. This project implements a
small clean-room adapter and does not claim to reproduce the synthesis/training
pipeline.
